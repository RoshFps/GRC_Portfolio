Sources for the BIA, continuity plan and tabletop exercise

Checked on 8 October 2026. Only the facts below are from public sources. Impact
scores, MTPD, RTO, RPO, volumes, recovery times, roles and the scenario are
made up.


Organisation and process

- Saarland University (ZPL): Fragen und Antworten A bis Z
  https://www.uni-saarland.de/en/einrichtung/zpl/studienorganisation/abisz.html
  Exam registration and results run in LSF, the deadline is shown at each exam,
  and late registration isn't possible where registration is mandatory.
  Used in: BIA (Process, Impact Over Time), continuity plan section 1.

- Saarland University, Systems Engineering: Prüfungsanmeldung und -termine
  https://www.uni-saarland.de/fachrichtung/systems-engineering/studium/pruefungsanmeldung-und-termine.html
  Online registration with the university account and iTAN, results in the
  HIS-Portal, and written registration at the exam office if online
  registration fails.
  Used in: BIA (Process, RTO & RPO), continuity plan step 4.

- Saarland University: Semestertermine Studienjahr 2026/2027 (PDF)
  https://www.uni-saarland.de/fileadmin/upload/dezernat/ls/semestertermine/Studienjahr2026-2027.pdf
  Winter term 2026/27 lectures run 12 October 2026 to 5 February 2027, the
  term ends 31 March 2027. The registration window dates are my assumption.
  Used in: BIA (Process, peak periods).

- Wikipedia: Saarland University
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saarland_University
  About 16,300 students, used to estimate registration volume.
  Used in: BIA (Process).


Standards

- ISO 22301:2019 Business continuity management systems
  https://www.iso.org/standard/75106.html
  BIA, MTPD, RTO and RPO (clause 8.2.2) and exercising (clause 8.5). Paid.

- BSI-Standard 200-4 Business Continuity Management
  https://www.bsi.bund.de/DE/Themen/Unternehmen-und-Organisationen/Standards-und-Zertifizierung/IT-Grundschutz/BSI-Standards/BSI-Standard-200-4-Business-Continuity-Management/bsi-standard-200-4_Business_Continuity_Management_node.html
  German BCM method, used as background. Free.

- ISO/IEC 27001:2022
  https://www.iso.org/standard/27001
  Annex A controls A.5.29 and A.5.30. Paid.


Law

- GDPR, Art. 33
  https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj
  72 hour breach notification, used in the plan and the exercise.
