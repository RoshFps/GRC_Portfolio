Continuity plan: exam registration and grade recording
Saarland University. Process owner: Head of Student Services. Version 0.1, October 2026.

Public facts are in sources.txt. Roles, numbers and procedures are made up.


1. Scope and targets

Covers exam registration, withdrawal, attendance lists and grade entry in LSF
(HIS-Portal) on both campuses. Targets come from the BIA
(saarland-university-bia.xlsx).

                     Peak (registration window, grading deadline)   Off-peak
  MTPD               2 days                                         4 weeks
  RTO                24 hours                                       1 week
  RPO                1 hour                                         24 hours


2. When to start the plan

- Peak period: students can't register, or examiners can't enter grades, for
  more than 2 hours.
- Any time: data in the exam database may be lost or tampered with.
- Off-peak: the outage is expected to last more than 2 working days.

The HIZ service desk or any exam office calls the Head of Student Services, who
starts the plan. If it looks like a cyber attack (risk R01), the CISO runs
incident response at the same time and decides on isolation first.


3. Who does what

- Head of Student Services (plan lead): starts the plan, runs the crisis call,
  decides on the paper fallback.
- Head of HIZ: restores LSF and the database, gives a recovery estimate every
  2 hours.
- CISO: leads incident response if there is an attack, decides when systems
  are clean.
- Vice President for Teaching: asks the exam boards to extend deadlines and
  signs the announcement.
- Exam office leads: run the paper fallback and keep a list of every manual
  registration.
- Press and Communications: website banner, student email, social media.
- Data Protection Officer: decides whether a breach has to be reported
  (GDPR Art. 33, 72 hours).


4. Steps

First hour
1. Plan lead opens a crisis call with HIZ, the CISO and the exam office leads.
2. HIZ confirms the cause and gives a first estimate. If an attack is
   suspected, nothing is restored until the CISO agrees.
3. Banner and email to students: "Exam registration is down. Your deadline is
   protected. Do not register twice."

Within 4 hours
4. If the estimate is longer than the RTO, switch to paper: exam offices take
   written registrations (paper form or signed scan by email) with a
   timestamp. The exam pages already allow this when online registration
   fails.
5. The Vice President for Teaching asks all exam board chairs to extend
   deadlines by the length of the outage plus 2 days.
6. Exam offices export the latest attendance lists they have, or rebuild them
   from the last backup.

Until it is fixed
7. HIZ restores LSF from the latest clean backup or moves to the standby site,
   with an update every 2 hours.
8. Plan lead checks the backlog every day against the MTPD.


5. Back to normal

- Before reopening, exam offices check a sample of registrations and grades
  against their own records, to spot data lost since the last backup or
  changed by an attacker.
- Paper registrations are entered with their original timestamp and each
  student gets a confirmation email.
- Announce that the outage is over and give the final deadlines.
- Within 2 weeks: lessons learned meeting, then update this plan, the BIA and
  risk R07.


6. Keeping the plan ready

Paper forms and the import template are kept in each exam office (BIA action
A5). The plan is tested once a year with tabletop-exercise.txt, before the
winter registration window. Contact details are kept in a separate offline
list.
