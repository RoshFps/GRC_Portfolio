Tabletop exercise: "Deadline Day"
Saarland University. Tests the continuity plan for exam registration. Planned for December 2026.

The scenario, times and numbers are made up.


Purpose

Check that the people in continuity-plan.txt can keep exam registration going
when LSF fails on the last two days of the winter registration window, and
whether the BIA targets (RTO 24 hours, RPO 1 hour at peak) are realistic with
today's backups.


Objectives

1. Start the plan in time and hold the first crisis call.
2. Decide on the paper fallback and deadline extensions within 4 hours.
3. Handle a suspected attack without restoring an infected system.
4. Agree what to tell students, and when.
5. Work out how much registration data would really be lost.


Setup

- 2 hours, discussion only, no systems touched.
- People: Head of Student Services (plan lead), Head of HIZ, CISO, Vice
  President for Teaching or deputy, two exam office leads, Press and
  Communications, Data Protection Officer.
- Run by the information security team, with one note taker.
- Rules: answer as you would on the day. No blame.


Scenario

The winter registration window closes on Friday 22 January 2027 at 23:59
(assumed date). About 40% of registrations usually come in the last three days.

Thu 21 Jan, 07:45
  The service desk gets calls: the HIS-Portal shows an error page. Two exam
  offices report the same. The LSF database server is not responding.
  Ask: Who is told first? Does this meet the trigger? Who opens the crisis
  call and who joins?

08:30
  HIZ finds the database files renamed with an unknown extension and a ransom
  note. Two file servers show the same.
  Ask: Who leads now, the plan lead or the CISO? What gets isolated? Is last
  night's backup safe to restore?

09:30
  The 02:00 backup is on the same storage system and its state is unknown.
  Restore takes 3 days if the backup is clean.
  Ask: Compare with RTO 24 hours and MTPD 2 days. Switch to paper now? What
  data is lost since 02:00?

11:00
  Students post screenshots online, the student council wants a statement and
  a regional newspaper calls.
  Ask: What do you tell students and the press? Who signs off the text? Is the
  extension announced now or later?

14:00
  Exam offices have queues and 400 paper forms already. One faculty has no
  paper form. Some students who registered on Wednesday evening say their
  registrations are gone.
  Ask: How are paper registrations recorded and entered later? How do you
  prove which online registrations existed?

Fri 22 Jan, 10:00
  Thursday's backup was encrypted too. Wednesday's is clean and the restore
  will finish Sunday evening. Logs suggest the attacker was inside for 10 days.
  Ask: Is personal data exposed? Does the DPO report within 72 hours? What is
  the new deadline? How do you check data before reopening?


What a good response looks like

- Plan started before 09:45 (2 hours after the first call).
- Nothing restored until the CISO confirms the backup is clean and the way in
  is closed.
- Paper fallback and one extension rule (outage plus 2 days) agreed by 11:45.
- One message to students, from one source, on website, email and social media.
- DPO involved by Friday 10:00 at the latest, with the Art. 33 decision written
  down.
- The group notices that nightly backups on the same storage can lose up to
  30 hours of registrations (Wednesday 02:00 to Thursday 07:45), far more than
  the 1 hour RPO.


Scoring

The note taker writes down, for each step: the decision, the time, who decided
and any gap in the plan. Each objective is scored:

  Met          done in time and as the plan says
  Partly met   done, but late or outside the plan
  Not met      not done, or the plan doesn't cover it


Afterwards

Within 2 weeks the facilitator writes a short report with findings and actions,
each with an owner and a due date. The findings go into continuity-plan.txt,
the BIA and the risk register (mainly R07, R01, R12 and R15).
