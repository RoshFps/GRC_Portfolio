Risk register mapped to BSI IT-Grundschutz and German rules

This replaces the TISAX mapping from my first plan. TISAX is for car industry
suppliers and doesn't fit a public university. What does apply:

- BSI IT-Grundschutz (BSI-Standard 200-2 and the Kompendium). HIS-HE 3/2025
  says Hamburg makes its Hochschulen follow it (p. 25) and Bavaria makes them
  run an ISMS (p. 17).

- Saarland Informationssicherheitsgesetz (IT-SiG SL), 15 May 2019. § 3 asks
  for appropriate technical and organisational measures and security
  concepts, and § 3 (2) says incidents must be reported to the Land's central
  IT provider. Nothing in §§ 1 to 8 mentions Hochschulen, an ISMS or
  Grundschutz. § 1 covers authorities and other public-law bodies under state
  supervision, so I think the university is covered, but the law doesn't say
  so directly. This needs checking with the university's legal office.

- NIS2 (Directive (EU) 2022/2555). Per HIS-HE (pp. 6 to 8) educational
  institutions are excluded from the research definition, and in November
  2023 the IT-Planungsrat asked not to extend the scope to them. So it's an
  open question whether a Hochschule is in scope. I didn't check later changes
  to German law.


About the table

Module names are from the Grundschutz Kompendium (2023). Most IDs were checked
against a public list on opensecurityarchitecture.org. These are from memory
and still need checking against the official PDF: SYS.3.1, SYS.3.2.x,
SYS.4.4, SYS.1.5, OPS.2.2, OPS.2.3, CON.8, NET.3.2, NET.3.3, APP.5.3, DER.3.1.
Which modules fit which risk is my own judgement, not an official mapping.


R01 Ransomware on central IT
  OPS.1.1.4 Schutz vor Schadprogrammen, CON.3 Datensicherungskonzept,
  DER.2.1 Behandlung von Sicherheitsvorfällen, DER.4 Notfallmanagement,
  OPS.1.1.3 Patch- und Änderungsmanagement
  Malware protection, offline backups, incident handling, restart planning.

R02 Credential phishing
  ORP.3 Sensibilisierung und Schulung, ORP.4 Identitäts- und
  Berechtigungsmanagement, APP.5.3 E-Mail-Client und -Server
  Awareness, strong login, mail filtering.

R03 Leak of research data
  CON.2 Datenschutz, CON.1 Kryptokonzept, ORP.4, CON.6 Löschen und Vernichten
  Classification, encryption, access, deletion.

R04 Lost or stolen laptops
  SYS.3.1 Laptops, SYS.2.1 Allgemeiner Client, CON.1, CON.7
  Informationssicherheit auf Auslandsreisen
  Hardening, disk encryption, travel rules.

R05 Supplier or uplink outage
  OPS.2.3 Nutzung von Outsourcing, OPS.2.2 Cloud-Nutzung, NET.1.1
  Netzarchitektur und -design, DER.4
  Supplier contracts, network redundancy, continuity plans.

R06 Unpatched institute servers
  OPS.1.1.3, SYS.1.1 Allgemeiner Server, ISMS.1 Sicherheitsmanagement,
  OPS.1.1.2 Ordnungsgemäße IT-Administration
  Asset inventory and patching for servers outside central IT.

R07 Campus management outage
  DER.4, SYS.1.1, SYS.1.5 Virtualisierung, INF.2 Rechenzentrum
  Redundancy and recovery of exam registration (see bcm/).

R08 Privileged account misuse
  OPS.1.1.2, ORP.4, OPS.1.1.5 Protokollierung
  Separate admin accounts, least privilege, logging.

R09 Accounts not removed
  ORP.4, ORP.2 Personal
  Joiner-mover-leaver process tied to HR and student records.

R10 Misconfigured file sharing
  CON.2, OPS.2.2, ORP.4
  Sharing rules for cloud and file shares.

R11 Espionage on research
  ISMS.1, DER.1 Detektion, NET.1.1
  Separate lab networks, detection.

R12 Data centre fire, water, power
  INF.2, INF.1 Allgemeines Gebäude, CON.3
  Physical protection and off-site backups.

R13 DDoS
  NET.1.1, NET.3.2 Firewall, DER.2.1
  Capacity, filtering at the uplink, incident process.

R14 Insecure in-house software
  CON.8 Software-Entwicklung, APP.3.1 Webanwendungen, OPS.1.1.6
  Software-Tests und -Freigaben
  Secure coding rules and testing.

R15 Attacks detected too late
  DER.1, OPS.1.1.5, DER.2.1
  Central logs, detection, incident process.

R16 Insecure lab and building devices
  SYS.4.4 Allgemeines IoT-Gerät, NET.1.1, OPS.1.1.3
  Network separation and patching.

R17 Loss of key IT staff
  ORP.1 Organisation, OPS.1.1.2, ORP.2
  Documentation, deputies, onboarding.

R18 Data in public AI tools
  ORP.1, CON.2, ORP.3
  Usage rules, data protection review, awareness.


Coverage

Every risk maps to at least two modules. Most used: ORP.4 (5 risks), NET.1.1
(4), then DER.2.1, DER.4, OPS.1.1.3, CON.2 and OPS.1.1.2 (3 each).
