## What this does and does not support

- Ransomware and data theft at German universities are documented every year since at least 2019, so a likelihood of 4 for R01 (ransomware) is defensible. The evidence shows the *sector*, not Saarland University.
- No public incident is listed for Saarland, which is the only point in the data that argues against a high likelihood for the university itself. It is absence of public reports, not evidence of absence.
- The LMU case is the closest match to register risks R07 and R03: a student administration system taken offline during enrolment and student master data probably copied.
- Most named incidents carry no attack type, so they cannot be split by risk (ransomware vs. data leak vs. DDoS) beyond what is written above.
