Likelihood review of the risk register

Done on 16 October 2026. For each risk I compared the likelihood in the
register (1 to 5) with what public data shows. The register itself is not
changed, so the scores and the dashboard still match. Data: incidents.txt and
output/kev-summary.txt.

Limits: the KEV catalogue is global and the incident counts cover the whole
sector, not Saarland. EPSS, NVD and the BSI and ENISA reports couldn't be
downloaded, so they aren't used. Where there is no data I say so.


R01 Ransomware on central IT (L = 4)
  361 of 1,748 KEV entries (21%) are linked to ransomware: 67 in edge and VPN
  products, 117 in Microsoft, 17 in virtualisation and file transfer.
  Hochschule Hannover, Ruhr West and Mainz all took IT offline after attacks.
  42 attacks reported to the BKA in 2022 to 2024.
  View: keep 4. No public incident at Saarland, so not 5.

R02 Credential phishing (L = 5)
  No university rate found. 390 Microsoft entries in KEV, 44 in the last year.
  View: keep, but indirect. 4 would also be fine.

R03 Leak of research data (L = 3)
  Universitätsbibliothek Leipzig, about 70,000 personal records (2022).
  View: keep 3.

R04 Lost or stolen laptops (L = 4)          No public data.
R05 Supplier or uplink outage (L = 2)       No public data.

R06 Unpatched institute servers (L = 4)
  265 edge and 107 web/mail entries in KEV, 59 and 20 added in the last year.
  View: keep 4.

R07 Campus management outage at peak (L = 2)
  LMU (2026): enrolment server broken into, enrolment stopped, deadlines
  extended. Ruhr West cancelled electronic exams (2023).
  View: the scenario is real. 2 is fine for Saarland, 3 would be too.

R08 Privileged account misuse (L = 2)       No public data.
R09 Accounts not removed (L = 4)            No public data.

R10 Misconfigured file sharing (L = 3)
  Only the Leipzig and LMU cases, neither stated to be a sharing mistake.
  View: keep 3.

R11 Espionage on research (L = 3)
  Nothing university specific read. Needs the BSI or Verfassungsschutz report.

R12 Data centre fire, water, power (L = 2)  No public data.

R13 DDoS (L = 3)
  FH Münster hit twice in January 2022.
  View: keep 3.

R14 Insecure in-house software (L = 3)
  KEV only covers third-party products. No direct data.

R15 Attacks detected too late (L = 4)       No public data.

R16 Insecure lab and building devices (L = 3)
  63 KEV entries in D-Link, TP-Link, NETGEAR, QNAP, MikroTik and Mitel
  products, 16 used by ransomware.
  View: keep 3.

R17 Loss of key IT staff (L = 3)
  HIS-HE: 3 of 8 science ministries offer no help with IT staffing.
  View: keep 3.

R18 Confidential data in public AI tools (L = 4)  No public data.


Result

8 of 18 likelihoods are backed, or at least not contradicted, by public data
(R01, R03, R06, R07, R10, R13, R16, R17). R02 is indirect. 9 have no public
data at all. So about half the register still rests on judgement. The next step
would be to use the university's own incident and scan data.
