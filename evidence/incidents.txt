Cyber incidents at German universities (public reports)

Collected on 16 October 2026 for the likelihood review. I only list what the
source actually says. Most sources give a date but not the attack type or how
long it lasted, so those are left out rather than guessed.


Overall figures

- 45 publicly known attacks on German Hochschulen (KonBriefing count, March
  2025). Source: HIS-HE Forum 3/2025, p. 4.
- 42 attacks on Hochschulen and research institutions reported to the BKA,
  2022 to 2024. Source: HIS-HE p. 4 and DFN Betriebstagung 2025 slides.
- About 20 reported incidents at Hochschulen per year. Source: DFN slides.
- 97.3% of university leaders rate the cyber threat as large or rather large
  (survey, summer 2024). Source: HIS-HE p. 1, citing Stifterverband.
- 53.4% of Hochschulen have emergency plans, at least for some areas.
  Source: HIS-HE p. 1.
- There is no central register of attacks and no general duty to report.
  Source: HIS-HE p. 5.
- Saarland: no publicly known incident in the KonBriefing list (March 2025).
  Source: HIS-HE pp. 5, 21, 29.

Caveats

- This shows the sector, not Saarland University. No public incident is
  listed for Saarland, but that means none was reported, not that none
  happened.
- Most entries have no attack type, so they can't be split by risk.
